## New in this version

- Added `surreal_trace()` to record each iteration of the search that makes a
  dataset.

  predictors and select the real ones one step at a time.
- Added `surreal_text_points()` and `surreal_image_points()` to return the
  points that `surreal_text()` and `surreal_image()` hide.
- `surreal()` is much faster and uses far less memory on large images.
- `surreal_app()` gained two tabs and three fixes.
